# How Devices Work content accuracy review

The repository contains 140 device entries (1–140), not 155. No missing devices were invented. Learner-facing references now describe devices and learning pages. Routes and category memberships were retained.

## Review and corrections

All 140 entries received a text review of their operating principle, signal path, working steps, component-function descriptions and quizzes. The CSV identifies every page individually. Authoritative references were checked for identified issues and relevant device families; this is not independent external validation of every sentence.

Corrections include temperature-sensor technologies, power versus sensing amplifiers, hydrophones, physical air filters, breathing valves, fluid reservoirs, command encoders, infrared receivers, gamma-event positioning, electrochemical electrodes, TOCO sensing, capacitor energy storage, MRI gradient drivers and magnetization, reconstruction computers, PCR heater drivers and heated lids, and recorder ADC/processor/memory/power functions. NFC transport is distinguished from payment tokenization; RFID credential handling is distinguished from biometric template extraction. The representative marine VHF radio now identifies transmit/receive switching.

24 pages have source-linked design-variant and measurement-limit explanations. These cover SpO₂ limitations, MRI, NFC, active/passive sonar, PET/SPECT, pump delivery limits, qPCR, inertial drift, ADS-B links, recorder functions, reaction-wheel saturation, magnetic heading/calibration, microstepping, EPIRB frequencies, Bluetooth variants, accelerometer readings, fingerprint capture types and LCD/OLED differences.

## Preservation and validation

All 140 device entries retain their component counts and control definitions. The VHF switch label changed without deleting a component. No diagrams, assets or simulations were removed. Existing How Devices Work tests plus three factual regression checks: 971 tests passed. Studio strict TypeScript and Vite production build passed. Browser inspection confirmed device wording and the INS source-linked Deep Dive.

## Limits

Image artwork and embedded image labels have not been independently fact-checked in this text review. Simulations remain simplified teaching models; automated tests check their implemented behavior, not equivalence to certified equipment. Existing broad or product-level references are retained; their presence alone does not substantiate every page claim.
