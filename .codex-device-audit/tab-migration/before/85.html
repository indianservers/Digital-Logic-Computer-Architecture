◈ Working Principle — Step by Step

  1. The driver generates a short optical pulse.

  2. Light travels toward the target at the speed of light.

  3. The surface returns a fraction of the incident light.

  4. The receiver lens focuses returned light on a photodetector.

  5. A TIA amplifies detector current and filtering reduces noise.

  6. A timing circuit measures round-trip delay; d = cΔt/2.

  7. Display distance and measurement quality.
