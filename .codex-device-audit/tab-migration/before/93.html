◈ Working Principle — Step by Step

  1. Accept local voice, video or digital data.

  2. Frame and code the payload.

  3. Create a modem waveform.

  4. The BUC translates and amplifies transmit RF.

  5. The dish radiates toward the selected satellite.

  6. LNB, modem and codec recover the downlink data.
