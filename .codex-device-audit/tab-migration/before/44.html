Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Pulse generator

    The implant contains sensing, timing, supervised pulse output, power and telemetry subsystems.

  2. 2. Leads

    Implanted leads connect sensing and output circuits to the cardiac electrode interface; sensing and capture are distinct checks.

  3. 3. Sensing amplifier

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  4. 4. Escape timer

    An intrinsic sensed beat resets the timer. If its programmed interval expires, the model schedules a pacing pulse.

  5. 5. Output stage

    A supervision state machine checks stored-energy or output readiness. This lab represents timing and status with a virtual event.

  6. 6. Battery

    The power source feeds regulated rails. Low supply voltage can reduce measurement reliability and must be detected by supervision.

  7. 7. Telemetry

    Telemetry connects to the acquisition or control chain. Cardiac sensing resets an escape timer when an intrinsic beat is detected. A pacing pulse is generated when the interval expires; effective capture depends on the electrode interface.

  8. 8. Connector header

    Connector header connects to the acquisition or control chain. Cardiac sensing resets an escape timer when an intrinsic beat is detected. A pacing pulse is generated when the interval expires; effective capture depends on the electrode interface.

Supervision: Lead capture failure invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
