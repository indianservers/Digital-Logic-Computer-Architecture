◈ Working Principle — Step by Step

  1. Collect weak satellite RF signals.

  2. Select the band with a low-noise RF front end.

  3. Translate the signal to an intermediate frequency.

  4. Match local spreading-code replicas with received samples.

  5. Estimate code arrival time including clock bias.

  6. Solve the satellite timing equations.

  7. Present position, time and navigation messages.
