Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Mouthpiece

    Mouthpiece connects to the acquisition or control chain. A flow sensor measures exhalation. Integrating flow estimates volume and permits comparison of the volume–time curve and flow–volume loop.

  2. 2. Flow sensor

    A pressure difference, turbine motion or thermal measurement estimates flow. Integrating flow over time gives volume.

  3. 3. Tubing

    Tubing connects to the acquisition or control chain. A flow sensor measures exhalation. Integrating flow estimates volume and permits comparison of the volume–time curve and flow–volume loop.

  4. 4. Pressure front end

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  5. 5. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  6. 6. MCU

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  7. 7. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

  8. 8. Memory

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

Supervision: Tube blocked invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.

Flow integration produces volume, FVC and timed expiratory measurements.

Flow–volume and volume–time curves reveal the effect of the modeled obstruction.
