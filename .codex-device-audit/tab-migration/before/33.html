◈Device Overview

Measure shifts in returning ultrasound frequency to estimate motion such as blood flow.

Key principle

Moving blood scatters ultrasound with a frequency shift. The relationship Δf = 2fv cos(θ)/c links measured shift to flow velocity and probe angle.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Transducer

    A transducer converts electrical drive into sound and returning sound into voltage. Round-trip acoustic delay carries range information.

  2. 2. Pulser

    Pulser connects to the acquisition or control chain. Moving blood scatters ultrasound with a frequency shift. The relationship Δf = 2fv cos(θ)/c links measured shift to flow velocity and probe angle.

  3. 3. T/R switch

    T/R switch connects to the acquisition or control chain. Moving blood scatters ultrasound with a frequency shift. The relationship Δf = 2fv cos(θ)/c links measured shift to flow velocity and probe angle.

  4. 4. Receive beamformer

    Timed delays align signals from multiple acoustic channels. Their sum emphasizes echoes arriving from a selected direction and depth.

  5. 5. I/Q demodulator

    Phase and frequency changes in returning echoes reveal motion along the beam. The estimated velocity depends on frequency and probe angle.

  6. 6. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  7. 7. Doppler DSP

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  8. 8. Color mapper

    Color mapper connects to the acquisition or control chain. Moving blood scatters ultrasound with a frequency shift. The relationship Δf = 2fv cos(θ)/c links measured shift to flow velocity and probe angle.

Supervision: Poor acoustic coupling invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.

◈Real-World Applications

Carotid-flow assessment

Frequency shifts estimate motion along the beam in a vessel.

Cardiac flow

Spectral and color displays represent blood-motion measurements.

◇

Fetal monitoring

Doppler activity can supply a fetal heart-rate signal.

Vascular assessment

Probe angle changes the relationship between velocity and observed shift.
