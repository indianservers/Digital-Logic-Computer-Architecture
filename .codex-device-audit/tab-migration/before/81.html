◈ Working Principle — Step by Step

  1. Synchronize waveform and receive timing.

  2. Illuminate a rotating search sector.

  3. RF pulses travel to a target and return as weak reflected echoes.

  4. Amplify weak reflected signals.

  5. Sample the receive waveform.

  6. Compare synthetic returns with an illustrative threshold.

  7. Plot teaching targets and scan coverage.

◈ Advantages, Limitations & Safety

• All targets and performance values are synthetic.

• Curvature, terrain and clutter limit line of sight.

• No operational targeting or engagement commands are modeled.

◈ Key Takeaways

✓ Transmit pulses and receive reflected echoes

✓ Round-trip delay determines range

✓ Doppler and scans support track association

✓ Identification uses a separate IFF interface

✓ Terrain, curvature and clutter constrain coverage
