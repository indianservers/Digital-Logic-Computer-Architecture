Induction Cooktop

Modern cooking. Powered by electromagnetism.

An induction cooktop uses a high-frequency electromagnetic field to heat compatible cookware directly.

◈ Block Diagram & Power Flow

Functional resonant inverter; coil current is a high-frequency teaching reference.

◈ How It Works — Step by Step

  1. Input protection and filtering precede the rectifier.

  2. Convert alternating input into a DC bus.

  3. Gate drives switch the power stage at high frequency.

  4. The coil creates an alternating magnetic field.

  5. Compatible cookware absorbs energy through induction.

  6. Absorbed power raises pan and food temperature.

  7. Sensor feedback disables incompatible pans or excessive temperatures.

◈ Simplified Circuit / Block Diagram

Half-bridge example: gate driver switches Q1/Q2 alternately; the work coil and capacitor form the resonant tank. Current and temperature feedback supervise heating.

◈ Safety Protections

✓ Pan detection inhibits incompatible or absent cookware.

✓ Temperature feedback disables heating above the modeled limit.

✓ Current and DC-bus supervision protect the power stage.

✓ A fan and heat sink cool power components.

◈ Key Takeaways

✓ Induction heats compatible cookware directly.

✓ Rectifier, inverter and coil form the power path.

✓ Pan coupling and thermal mass affect heating.

✓ Pan detection and temperature protection supervise operation.
