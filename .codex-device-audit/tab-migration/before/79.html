◈ Working Principle — Step by Step

  1. Rotate the antenna through the coverage region.

  2. Send a timed radar waveform.

  3. Collect target echoes and ground clutter.

  4. Convert round-trip delay into distance.

  5. Estimate radial speed from Doppler frequency.

  6. Associate synthetic detections over successive scans.
