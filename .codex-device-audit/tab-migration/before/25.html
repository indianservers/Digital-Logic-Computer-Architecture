Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Ultrasound transducer

    A transducer converts electrical drive into sound and returning sound into voltage. Round-trip acoustic delay carries range information.

  2. 2. TOCO sensor

    TOCO sensor connects to the acquisition or control chain. A Doppler probe estimates fetal heart activity while a TOCO transducer measures relative uterine activity. Separate conditioned channels support trend displays and time-aligned recordings.

  3. 3. Analog front end

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  4. 4. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  5. 5. Heart-rate DSP

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  6. 6. Audio output

    A digital-to-analog interface and transducer reproduce the processed audio waveform. Output limits and gain shape the audible signal.

  7. 7. LCD

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

  8. 8. Recorder

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

Supervision: Poor probe contact invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
