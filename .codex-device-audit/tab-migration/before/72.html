◈System Overview

GNSS position and avionics data are combined with identity and encoded for automatic broadcast. This example uses the 1090 MHz extended-squitter path. Receivers on aircraft and ground stations decode traffic; broadcast quality depends on valid navigation data.

Broadcast aircraft position and identity derived from navigation and onboard data.

◈How It Works — 6 Steps

  1. Obtain position, velocity and time from GNSS.

  2. Add pressure altitude, identity and status.

  3. Format extended-squitter message data.

  4. Transmit without requiring an interrogation.

  5. Aircraft and ground stations receive the broadcast.

  6. Decode and plot nearby aircraft.

◈Real-World Applications

  • Traffic awareness
  • Air traffic services
  • Search and rescue
  • Weather / operational awareness
  • Airspace modernization

◈Safety / Limitations

  • Position and integrity depend on the navigation source.
  • Traffic displays complement collision avoidance systems.
  • ADS-B In and Out serve different roles.

◈Key Takeaways

  • GNSS position / time starts the signal path.
  • 1090 MHz transmitter supports Top antenna broadcast.
  • Valid acquisition and supervision are required for trustworthy outputs.
