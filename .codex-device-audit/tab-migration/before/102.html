Lab 102 — Automatic Identification System (AIS)

Track Vessels · Enhance Safety · A Safer Sea for Everyone

AIS combines GNSS position/time with vessel identity and motion data. An encoder frames digital messages and a VHF transceiver sends and receives them on AIS channels, while the display and NMEA interfaces present nearby vessels.

◈ Key Takeaways

✓ AIS identifies and tracks vessels using VHF

✓ GNSS and onboard data supply position and identity

✓ Standard messages use GMSK modulation at 9.6 kbps

✓ Received reports appear as selectable chart targets

✓ NMEA interfaces share data with navigation equipment

✓ Vessel reports support situational awareness

✓ All vessel identities are synthetic.

✓ No operational AIS transmission occurs.

✓ AIS reports may age or be absent.
