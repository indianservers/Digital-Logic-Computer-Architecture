◈ Working Principle — Step by Step

  1. Convert sound into an electrical input.

  2. The audio codec creates digital samples.

  3. Encrypt and authenticate a framed message.

  4. Baseband logic prepares symbols and framing.

  5. The RF chain would send the modulated frame.

  6. A remote receiver would demodulate the frame.

  7. Authenticate before releasing plaintext.

  8. Reconstruct the recovered audio or data.
