Lab 118 — LiDAR Mapping Robot

Build. Map. Navigate. Explore the world with LiDAR.

A 2D LiDAR measures range at successive scan angles. IMU and wheel odometry estimate motion; transforming each ray into world coordinates updates an occupancy grid.

◈ Working Principle (Step by Step)

  1. Cast a synthetic ray and measure its first intersection.

  2. Record scan angle and range for each ray.

  3. Convert ranges to robot/world Cartesian coordinates.

  4. Mark traversed cells free and endpoints occupied.

  5. Search the occupancy grid for a route to the goal.

  6. Follow the route with bounded speed.

  7. New poses reveal additional cells.
