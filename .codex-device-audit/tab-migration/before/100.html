Lab 100 — Depth Sounder

Explore how underwater depth is measured using sound

An echo sounder emits an acoustic pulse and measures its round-trip travel time to the seabed. Depth is c × time / 2.

◈ Working Principle (Step by Step)

  1. Send a short virtual drive pulse.

  2. The transducer produces sound pressure.

  3. The seabed returns an echo.

  4. The transducer converts the echo to voltage.

  5. Amplify, filter and digitize.

  6. Estimate round-trip echo time.

  7. Multiply delay by calibrated sound speed / 2.

  8. Show depth and the synthetic seabed profile.
