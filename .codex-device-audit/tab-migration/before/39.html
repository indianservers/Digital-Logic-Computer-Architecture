◈Device Overview

Use pressure and flow feedback to regulate the delivery of breathing support.

Key principle

The controller combines commanded breath settings with measured flow and pressure. In this lung model, pressure = PEEP + volume/compliance + resistance × flow.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Air inlet filter

    Bandwidth limiting removes unwanted components before sampling. An anti-alias filter suppresses frequencies above the acquisition bandwidth.

  2. 2. Blower / turbine

    The air delivery stage establishes pressure and flow. A controller compares sensor feedback with the selected pressure or volume target.

  3. 3. Oxygen mixer

    Gas-routing components set mixture and circulation. Sensors supervise oxygen, flow, pressure and the breathing circuit's condition.

  4. 4. Inspiratory valve

    The pneumatic stage changes cuff pressure. Slow deflation exposes pulse-related oscillations whose envelope supports an oscillometric estimate.

  5. 5. Flow sensor

    A pressure difference, turbine motion or thermal measurement estimates flow. Integrating flow over time gives volume.

  6. 6. Pressure sensor

    A transducer converts pressure or force into an electrical signal. Conditioning and calibration produce a supervised mechanical measurement.

  7. 7. Expiratory valve

    The pneumatic stage changes cuff pressure. Slow deflation exposes pulse-related oscillations whose envelope supports an oscillometric estimate.

  8. 8. Humidifier

    Controlled heating adds moisture to the gas stream. Temperature and water-level supervision support the breathing circuit.

  9. 9. Controller

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

Supervision: Airway obstruction invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
