◈ Working Principle — Step by Step

  1. Encode digital voice and data into modem symbols.

  2. Convert the transmit signal to RF and amplify it.

  3. Track the selected satellite despite aircraft motion.

  4. The satellite relay forwards the signal to a ground station.

  5. The antenna collects the weak satellite downlink.

  6. The LNA adds low-noise gain before downconversion.

  7. Downconvert, demodulate and check recovered messages.
