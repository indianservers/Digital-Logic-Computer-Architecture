◈ Working Principle — Step by Step

  1. Measure local magnetic field components.

  2. Filter sensor outputs.

  3. Sample the three magnetic axes.

  4. Use the tilt estimate with magnetic measurements.

  5. Subtract the selected hard-iron offset.

  6. Rotate into the horizontal plane and evaluate atan2.

  7. Normalize heading into 0–360 degrees.
