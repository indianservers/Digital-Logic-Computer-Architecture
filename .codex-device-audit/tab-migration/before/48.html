Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Blower

    The air delivery stage establishes pressure and flow. A controller compares sensor feedback with the selected pressure or volume target.

  2. 2. Pressure sensor

    A transducer converts pressure or force into an electrical signal. Conditioning and calibration produce a supervised mechanical measurement.

  3. 3. Flow sensor

    A pressure difference, turbine motion or thermal measurement estimates flow. Integrating flow over time gives volume.

  4. 4. Breath-phase detector

    Breath-phase detector connects to the acquisition or control chain. Breath-phase detection switches the pressure target between IPAP and EPAP. Pressure support is their difference; flow and pressure feedback supervise delivery.

  5. 5. Controller

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  6. 6. Humidifier

    Controlled heating adds moisture to the gas stream. Temperature and water-level supervision support the breathing circuit.

  7. 7. Tubing

    Tubing connects to the acquisition or control chain. Breath-phase detection switches the pressure target between IPAP and EPAP. Pressure support is their difference; flow and pressure feedback supervise delivery.

  8. 8. Mask

    Mask connects to the acquisition or control chain. Breath-phase detection switches the pressure target between IPAP and EPAP. Pressure support is their difference; flow and pressure feedback supervise delivery.

Supervision: Mask leak invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.

◈Real-World Applications

✦

Bilevel respiratory support

Separate inspiratory and expiratory targets create a pressure-support difference.

◉

Noninvasive ventilation

A mask interface delivers pressure while leak and flow are supervised.

◇

Ventilatory assistance

Breath-phase timing determines when the controller switches pressure targets.
