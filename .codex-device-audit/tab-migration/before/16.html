Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. IR optics

    IR optics connects to the acquisition or control chain. A thermopile detects emitted thermal radiation. Ambient compensation and emissivity assumptions affect the conversion from received energy to surface temperature.

  2. 2. Thermopile

    Infrared radiation warms one set of thermocouple junctions relative to a reference. Their combined voltage depends on received radiant power.

  3. 3. Ambient sensor

    Ambient sensor connects to the acquisition or control chain. A thermopile detects emitted thermal radiation. Ambient compensation and emissivity assumptions affect the conversion from received energy to surface temperature.

  4. 4. Amplifier

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  5. 5. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  6. 6. Compensation MCU

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  7. 7. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

  8. 8. Battery

    The power source feeds regulated rails. Low supply voltage can reduce measurement reliability and must be detected by supervision.

Supervision: Thermopile fault invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.

◈Real-World Applications

✦

Non-contact screening

Non-contact optical measurement depends on emissivity, field filling and ambient compensation.

◉

Surface measurements

The sensor estimates a surface temperature rather than directly measuring internal temperature.

◇

Temperature inspection

Changing distance or surface properties can shift the radiant-temperature estimate.
