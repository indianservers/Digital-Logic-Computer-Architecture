Lab 124 — Wi-Fi Router

Explore the hardware, architecture and working of a modern Wi-Fi router.

Ethernet PHYs receive frames from WAN and LAN links. A router SoC performs packet forwarding, NAT, firewall rules and queue management; its switch fabric and wireless MAC/PHY send traffic to selected interfaces.

◈ Working Principle (Step by Step)

  1. Ethernet PHY receives upstream frames.

  2. SoC applies route, NAT, firewall and queue decisions.

  3. Forward frames toward the intended interface.

  4. MAC scheduling and PHY modulation drive the RF chain.

  5. Decode incoming frames from associated clients.

  6. Share airtime and forward client traffic.

  7. Send packets toward LAN clients or the WAN.
