◈Device Overview

Measure an electrochemical reaction on a test strip and convert current into a glucose estimate.

Key principle

An enzyme-coated electrochemical strip produces a concentration-related current. A current-to-voltage front end, ADC and calibration model estimate glucose concentration.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Test strip

    An enzyme-mediated electrochemical reaction creates a concentration-dependent current when a sufficient sample wets the strip.

  2. 2. Electrochemical electrodes

    Conductive contacts acquire a differential biopotential or provide the supervised output interface. Contact impedance affects signal quality.

  3. 3. Current front end

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  4. 4. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  5. 5. MCU

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  6. 6. Calibration memory

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

  7. 7. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

  8. 8. Battery

    The power source feeds regulated rails. Low supply voltage can reduce measurement reliability and must be detected by supervision.

Supervision: Insufficient sample invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
