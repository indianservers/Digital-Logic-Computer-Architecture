Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Translate a programmed infusion rate into precise linear movement of a syringe plunger. The Syringe Pump signal path begins with rate setting and leads to delivery supervision. Between these endpoints, mcu / motion plan, motor drive, lead screw / plunger, position / force sensing connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

  1. 1. Syringe barrel

    Syringe barrel connects to the acquisition or control chain. Motor rotation advances a lead screw and syringe plunger. Syringe cross-sectional area relates plunger travel to delivered volume.

  2. 2. Plunger

    Plunger connects to the acquisition or control chain. Motor rotation advances a lead screw and syringe plunger. Syringe cross-sectional area relates plunger travel to delivered volume.

  3. 3. Lead screw

    A driver converts a controller command into motion. Encoder, force or current feedback closes the loop and reveals stalled or overloaded operation.

  4. 4. Motor / gears

    A driver converts a controller command into motion. Encoder, force or current feedback closes the loop and reveals stalled or overloaded operation.

  5. 5. Position encoder

    Position sensing reports mechanical motion to the controller. Comparing desired and observed position produces the servo error.

  6. 6. Force sensor

    A transducer converts pressure or force into an electrical signal. Conditioning and calibration produce a supervised mechanical measurement.

  7. 7. MCU

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  8. 8. Battery

    The power source feeds regulated rails. Low supply voltage can reduce measurement reliability and must be detected by supervision.

Supervision: Plunger occluded invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
