Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Compare red and infrared light absorption to estimate blood oxygen saturation and pulse rate. The Pulse Oximeter signal path begins with red / ir leds and leads to spo₂ display. Between these endpoints, finger tissue, photodiode, analog front end, adc / ratio processing connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

Red and infrared light have different absorption responses in oxygenated and deoxygenated blood. Pulsatile AC/DC ratios at the two wavelengths form a calibrated ratio-of-ratios estimate.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Red LED

    The controller alternates red and infrared illumination. Separate optical samples allow a ratio of normalized pulsatile absorbances.

  2. 2. Infrared LED

    The controller alternates red and infrared illumination. Separate optical samples allow a ratio of normalized pulsatile absorbances.

  3. 3. Photodiode

    Incoming light produces an electrical response. Optical conversion or gain occurs before the analog signal is conditioned and sampled.

  4. 4. Transimpedance amplifier

    An amplifier with feedback resistance converts a small sensor current into a measurable voltage: Vout is proportional to input current.

  5. 5. Analog filter

    Bandwidth limiting removes unwanted components before sampling. An anti-alias filter suppresses frequencies above the acquisition bandwidth.

  6. 6. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  7. 7. DSP

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  8. 8. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

Supervision: Low perfusion invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
