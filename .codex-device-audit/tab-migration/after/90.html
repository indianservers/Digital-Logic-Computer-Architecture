Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Gyroscopes estimate changing attitude and accelerometers estimate specific force. Strapdown navigation transforms force into a navigation frame, compensates gravity and integrates velocity and position. This reduced horizontal model shows acceleration bias accumulating into quadratic position error; aiding attenuates the illustrative bias.

Integrate measured rotation and acceleration to estimate orientation and movement. The Inertial Navigation System signal path begins with gyroscopes / accelerometers and leads to position / attitude. Between these endpoints, sensor readout, calibration, attitude integration, navigation filter connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈ Working Principle — Step by Step

  1. Sample acceleration and angular rate.

  2. Filter and compensate sensor offsets.

  3. Digitize synchronized sensor channels.

  4. Integrate angular rate into orientation.

  5. Transform acceleration and integrate velocity and position.

  6. Publish the reduced navigation solution and validity.
