Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Coordinate joint movement with force feedback and supervisory safety monitoring. The Collaborative Robot signal path begins with task target and leads to motion feedback. Between these endpoints, kinematics / trajectory, joint servo control, torque / position sensors, safety monitoring connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

Detailed Operating Principle

A collaborative robot combines joint motors, reduction gears, encoders and force/torque sensing. The controller plans motion while monitoring speed, contact force and safety inputs. Teach mode records joint waypoints for local replay. A cobot’s safety depends on the entire application; force and speed limits in this reduced lab are illustrative.

◈ Working Principle (Step by Step)

  1. Read joint positions, interaction force and nearby presence.

  2. Apply motion targets and active limits.

  3. Servo channels drive motors using encoder feedback.

  4. Reduce demand when a person approaches in monitored mode.

  5. Move the end effector toward the teaching target.

  6. Stop virtual motion when force exceeds the limit.

  7. Record the requested joint positions locally.

  8. Follow recorded waypoints under the same limits.
