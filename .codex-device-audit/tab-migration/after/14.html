Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Convert focused light into pixel values and process them into a digital image. The Digital Camera signal path begins with light / lens and leads to image / video. Between these endpoints, image sensor, pixel readout, adc, image signal processor connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

Exposure depends on aperture, exposure time and scene light. A sensor accumulates charge, an ADC samples pixels and an image processor reconstructs color and contrast.

  6. 6. Image signal processor
