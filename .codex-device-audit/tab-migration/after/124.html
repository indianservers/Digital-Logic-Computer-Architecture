Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Ethernet PHYs receive frames from WAN and LAN links. A router SoC performs packet forwarding, NAT, firewall rules and queue management; its switch fabric and wireless MAC/PHY send traffic to selected interfaces. RF front ends and antennas convert wireless data into radio signals. The reduced throughput model illustrates shared airtime, signal loss and channel interference.

Receive network frames, route packets and transmit them through wired or wireless interfaces. The Wi-Fi Router signal path begins with ethernet / wi-fi input and leads to network interface. Between these endpoints, phy / mac, packet buffers, routing / switching, output queues connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈ Working Principle (Step by Step)

  1. Ethernet PHY receives upstream frames.

  2. SoC applies route, NAT, firewall and queue decisions.

  3. Forward frames toward the intended interface.

  4. MAC scheduling and PHY modulation drive the RF chain.

  5. Decode incoming frames from associated clients.

  6. Share airtime and forward client traffic.

  7. Send packets toward LAN clients or the WAN.
