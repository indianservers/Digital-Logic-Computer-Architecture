Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Generate timed stimulation pulses with controlled amplitude and output supervision. The TENS Machine signal path begins with pulse settings and leads to output monitoring. Between these endpoints, timing mcu, pulse generator, output driver, electrode interface connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

A timing controller produces low-frequency pulse trains. Pulse width, repetition rate and current control determine the modeled electrical waveform at the electrode interface.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Microcontroller

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  2. 2. Timing generator

    The timer sets pulse repetition and microsecond pulse widths, with positive and negative phases separated by an interval.

  3. 3. Current-controlled output

    A controlled output stage regulates the modeled pulse amplitude across the electrode interface.

  4. 4. Lead wires

    Paired leads connect the supervised output to the electrode interface; disconnection invalidates delivery.

  5. 5. Electrodes

    Paired leads connect the supervised output to the electrode interface; disconnection invalidates delivery.

  6. 6. Battery

    The power source feeds regulated rails. Low supply voltage can reduce measurement reliability and must be detected by supervision.

  7. 7. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

Supervision: Electrode disconnected invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.

◈Circuit View

Microcontroller → Timing generator → Current-controlled output → Lead wires → Electrodes → Battery → Display
Functional architecture; power and safety supervision are separate subsystems.

The sensor interface conditions the analog signal. Sampling and digital processing extract the device’s useful output.
