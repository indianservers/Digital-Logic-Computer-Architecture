Operating Principle & Engineering Context

A GNSS receiver estimates position, velocity and time. The navigation processor overlays the solution on a stored digital chart, calculates waypoint bearing and distance, and exchanges data through NMEA interfaces. The chart and route in this lab are synthetic; waypoints can be edited locally.

Place a GNSS position estimate onto a digital marine chart with navigation overlays. The GPS Chartplotter signal path begins with satellite signals and leads to navigation display. Between these endpoints, gnss receiver, position solution, digital chart database, chart / route rendering connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈ Working Principle (Step by Step)

  1. The antenna collects GNSS radio signals.

  2. Correlate codes and solve position / clock.

  3. Retrieve local chart data.

  4. Plot own position and depth reference.

  5. Create waypoints and calculate legs.

  6. Update distance, bearing and route progress.
