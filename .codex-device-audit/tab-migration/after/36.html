Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Acquire high-resolution X-ray projections with a dedicated breast-imaging detector system. The Mammography System signal path begins with controlled x-ray source and leads to mammogram. Between these endpoints, tissue attenuation, digital detector, pixel readout, image correction connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

Controlled breast geometry and a dedicated X-ray spectrum support projection imaging. A digital detector captures transmitted radiation; exposure, thickness and image correction affect contrast and noise.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. X-ray tube

    Accelerated electrons strike a target to produce X-rays. Tube voltage controls the spectrum and exposure settings influence photon count.

  2. 2. Compression assembly

    Compression assembly connects to the acquisition or control chain. Controlled breast geometry and a dedicated X-ray spectrum support projection imaging. A digital detector captures transmitted radiation; exposure, thickness and image correction affect contrast and noise.

  3. 3. Collimator

    Geometric apertures constrain the directions reaching or leaving the imaging system. In gamma imaging, they trade sensitivity for spatial resolution.

  4. 4. Detector

    Detector connects to the acquisition or control chain. Controlled breast geometry and a dedicated X-ray spectrum support projection imaging. A digital detector captures transmitted radiation; exposure, thickness and image correction affect contrast and noise.

  5. 5. Readout / ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  6. 6. Image processor

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  7. 7. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

  8. 8. Safety interlocks

    Input protection and interlocks supervise the interface and prevent invalid states from propagating to the controlled output.

Supervision: Detector fault invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
