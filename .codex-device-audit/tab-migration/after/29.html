Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Convert strain in load cells into a calibrated digital weight reading. The Digital Weighing Scale signal path begins with applied force and leads to weight display. Between these endpoints, load cell bridge, instrumentation amplifier, adc, calibration mcu connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

Strain gauges change resistance under load. A Wheatstone bridge produces a small differential voltage that is amplified, digitized, calibrated and corrected by tare.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Load cells

    Strain changes the resistance of bridge elements. The small differential bridge voltage is proportional to applied load after calibration.

  2. 2. Wheatstone bridge

    Strain changes the resistance of bridge elements. The small differential bridge voltage is proportional to applied load after calibration.

  3. 3. Instrumentation amplifier

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  4. 4. High-resolution ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  5. 5. MCU

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  6. 6. Tare logic

    Tare logic connects to the acquisition or control chain. Strain gauges change resistance under load. A Wheatstone bridge produces a small differential voltage that is amplified, digitized, calibrated and corrected by tare.

  7. 7. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

  8. 8. Battery

    The power source feeds regulated rails. Low supply voltage can reduce measurement reliability and must be detected by supervision.

Supervision: Load-cell fault invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.

◈Circuit View

A differential bridge signal is amplified before conversion. Excitation and reference must be stable.
