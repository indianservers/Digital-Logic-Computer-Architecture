Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Use pressure feedback to regulate a blower and maintain a prescribed continuous airway pressure. The CPAP Machine signal path begins with pressure setting and leads to airway pressure feedback. Between these endpoints, pressure sensor, adc, pressure controller, blower connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

A pressure controller adjusts blower drive to maintain a continuous airway-pressure target. Flow estimation, mask leaks and humidification affect system behaviour.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Air filter

    Bandwidth limiting removes unwanted components before sampling. An anti-alias filter suppresses frequencies above the acquisition bandwidth.

  2. 2. Blower

    The air delivery stage establishes pressure and flow. A controller compares sensor feedback with the selected pressure or volume target.

  3. 3. Pressure sensor

    A transducer converts pressure or force into an electrical signal. Conditioning and calibration produce a supervised mechanical measurement.

  4. 4. Flow sensor

    A pressure difference, turbine motion or thermal measurement estimates flow. Integrating flow over time gives volume.

  5. 5. Control board

    Control board connects to the acquisition or control chain. A pressure controller adjusts blower drive to maintain a continuous airway-pressure target. Flow estimation, mask leaks and humidification affect system behaviour.

  6. 6. Humidifier

    Controlled heating adds moisture to the gas stream. Temperature and water-level supervision support the breathing circuit.

  7. 7. Tubing

    Tubing connects to the acquisition or control chain. A pressure controller adjusts blower drive to maintain a continuous airway-pressure target. Flow estimation, mask leaks and humidification affect system behaviour.

  8. 8. Mask

    Mask connects to the acquisition or control chain. A pressure controller adjusts blower drive to maintain a continuous airway-pressure target. Flow estimation, mask leaks and humidification affect system behaviour.

Supervision: Mask disconnected invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
