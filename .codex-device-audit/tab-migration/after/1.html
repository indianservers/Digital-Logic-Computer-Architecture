Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Convert temperature-dependent sensor readings into a calibrated digital measurement. The Digital Thermometer signal path begins with temperature and leads to display. Between these endpoints, thermistor, signal conditioning, adc, calibration mcu connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

An NTC thermistor’s resistance decreases as temperature rises. A calibrated divider voltage is sampled by an ADC and converted to temperature with a resistance–temperature curve.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Thermistor

    Temperature changes resistance. A stable excitation and calibration curve convert that resistance into a temperature estimate.

  2. 2. Voltage divider

    The sensor and a known resistor divide a stable supply voltage. The resulting voltage represents the sensor resistance.

  3. 3. Signal-conditioning amplifier

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  4. 4. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  5. 5. Microcontroller

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  6. 6. Coin-cell battery

    The power source feeds regulated rails. Low supply voltage can reduce measurement reliability and must be detected by supervision.

  7. 7. LCD

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

Supervision: Sensor disconnected invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.

◈Circuit View

10 kΩ NTC + 10 kΩ divider → buffered voltage → 12-bit ADC. Temperature is recovered using the β equation.
