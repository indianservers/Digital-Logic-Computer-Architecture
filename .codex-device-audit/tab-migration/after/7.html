Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Combine X-ray projections collected around the body into reconstructed cross-sectional images. The CT Scanner signal path begins with rotating x-ray source and leads to slice image. Between these endpoints, detector array, data acquisition, adc, tomographic reconstruction connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

An X-ray source and detector acquire projections around the object. Reconstruction combines attenuation measurements into cross-sectional images.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Rotating X-ray source

    Accelerated electrons strike a target to produce X-rays. Tube voltage controls the spectrum and exposure settings influence photon count.

  2. 2. Detector array

    An array samples radiation across spatial positions. Pixel correction and readout convert those local responses into usable projection or image data.

  3. 3. Gantry

    Gantry connects to the acquisition or control chain. An X-ray source and detector acquire projections around the object. Reconstruction combines attenuation measurements into cross-sectional images.

  4. 4. Slip ring

    Slip ring connects to the acquisition or control chain. An X-ray source and detector acquire projections around the object. Reconstruction combines attenuation measurements into cross-sectional images.

  5. 5. Acquisition electronics

    Acquisition electronics connects to the acquisition or control chain. An X-ray source and detector acquire projections around the object. Reconstruction combines attenuation measurements into cross-sectional images.

  6. 6. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  7. 7. Reconstruction computer

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  8. 8. Patient table

    Patient table connects to the acquisition or control chain. An X-ray source and detector acquire projections around the object. Reconstruction combines attenuation measurements into cross-sectional images.

Supervision: Detector-channel fault invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
