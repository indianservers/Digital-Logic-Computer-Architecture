Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Carry illumination and optical images through an endoscope to a digital video processor. The Endoscope Camera System signal path begins with illumination and leads to clinical video display. Between these endpoints, endoscope optics, image sensor, video acquisition, image processor connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.
