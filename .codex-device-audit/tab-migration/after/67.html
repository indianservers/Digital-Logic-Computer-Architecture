Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Gyroscopes and accelerometers estimate attitude; barometer and GNSS support altitude and navigation. Attitude and rate loops convert desired motion into torque requests, and a motor mixer allocates those requests to ESCs. This reduced-order four-motor model demonstrates feedback and saturation.

Fuse motion sensors and guidance targets to coordinate multiple motor speeds. The Drone Flight Controller signal path begins with imu / navigation sensors and leads to vehicle feedback. Between these endpoints, state estimation, flight controller, motor mixing, escs / motors connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈How It Works — 6 Steps

  1. Acquire IMU, barometer, GNSS and radio commands.

  2. Estimate attitude and position from complementary measurements.

  3. Form desired attitude, rate or altitude targets.

  4. Mix thrust and torque requests into motor outputs.

  5. Correct errors using measured motion.

  6. Supervise battery, link and sensor validity.

◈Safety / Limitations

  • Link loss and low battery require configured failsafe responses.
  • Saturated motors reduce available control authority.
  • The example is a software teaching model.

◈Key Takeaways

  • IMU angular rate starts the signal path.
  • Failsafe supervision supports ESC commands.
  • Valid acquisition and supervision are required for trustworthy outputs.
