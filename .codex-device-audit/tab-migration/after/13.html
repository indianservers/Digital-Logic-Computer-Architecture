Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Process returning radio echoes to estimate object range, direction or motion. The Surveillance Radar signal path begins with rf transmitter and leads to radar display. Between these endpoints, antenna, echo receiver, adc, range / doppler processing connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

Radio pulses reflect from objects. Time delay supports range estimation and frequency shift supports motion estimation; antenna steering determines observation direction.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Pulse generator

    A timing state machine schedules pulses or watches for a sensed event. Period, width and event ordering define the output sequence.

  2. 2. Transmitter

    The RF interface transmits or receives electromagnetic energy. Matching, switching and demodulation connect the physical link to digital processing.

  3. 3. Antenna

  5. 5. Receiver

    The RF interface transmits or receives electromagnetic energy. Matching, switching and demodulation connect the physical link to digital processing.

  6. 6. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  7. 7. Range / Doppler processor

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  8. 8. Tracker

    Tracker connects to the acquisition or control chain. Radio pulses reflect from objects. Time delay supports range estimation and frequency shift supports motion estimation; antenna steering determines observation direction.

Supervision: Receiver fault invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
