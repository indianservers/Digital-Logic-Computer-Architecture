Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Track pulse-related changes in reflected light to calculate heart rate. The Heart Rate Monitor signal path begins with led illumination and leads to heart-rate display. Between these endpoints, tissue reflection, photodiode, adc, pulse detection connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Green LEDs

    Green LEDs connects to the acquisition or control chain. Optical PPG senses pulsatile absorption changes, while a chest-band ECG senses electrical activity. Filtering and beat detection estimate rate; motion and contact quality affect confidence.

  2. 2. Photodiode

    Incoming light produces an electrical response. Optical conversion or gain occurs before the analog signal is conditioned and sampled.

  3. 3. Analog front end

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  4. 4. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  5. 5. Heart-rate processor

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  6. 6. Bluetooth

    Bluetooth connects to the acquisition or control chain. Optical PPG senses pulsatile absorption changes, while a chest-band ECG senses electrical activity. Filtering and beat detection estimate rate; motion and contact quality affect confidence.

  7. 7. Battery

    The power source feeds regulated rails. Low supply voltage can reduce measurement reliability and must be detected by supervision.

  8. 8. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

Supervision: Poor skin contact invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
