Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Translate a programmed delivery rate into controlled motor motion with sensor-based supervision. The Infusion Pump signal path begins with rate settings and leads to alarm / display. Between these endpoints, control mcu, motor driver, pump mechanism, flow / pressure feedback connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

A motor moves a pumping mechanism at a controlled rate. Flow supervision, pressure sensing and air detection protect the delivery path.
