Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Detect emitted photons and reconstruct the distribution of an administered imaging tracer. The PET Scanner signal path begins with tracer emission and leads to functional image. Between these endpoints, scintillation detectors, photodetector readout, energy / timing processing, tomographic reconstruction connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

A tracer produces annihilation photons detected in coincidence. Lines of response and corrections are combined to reconstruct a tracer-distribution image.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Detector ring

    An array samples radiation across spatial positions. Pixel correction and readout convert those local responses into usable projection or image data.

  2. 2. Scintillation crystals

    Incident radiation deposits energy in a scintillator, which emits optical photons for subsequent detection.

  3. 3. SiPM / PMT

    Incoming light produces an electrical response. Optical conversion or gain occurs before the analog signal is conditioned and sampled.

  4. 4. Coincidence electronics

    Coincidence electronics connects to the acquisition or control chain. A tracer produces annihilation photons detected in coincidence. Lines of response and corrections are combined to reconstruct a tracer-distribution image.

  5. 5. ADC / timing

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  6. 6. Correction computer

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  7. 7. Reconstruction

    A mathematical reconstruction combines spatially encoded measurements or multiple projections into an image. Acquisition quality limits the result.

  8. 8. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

Supervision: Coincidence-channel fault invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
