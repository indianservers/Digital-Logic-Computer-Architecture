Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Coordinate optical pulses with power measurements, cooling and interlocks. The Medical Laser System signal path begins with treatment settings and leads to interlock status. Between these endpoints, timing controller, laser source, optical delivery, power / thermal feedback connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

Optical energy is delivered in controlled pulses or continuous output. Source control, cooling, power sensing and interlocks supervise optical delivery.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Laser source

    The driven laser medium generates coherent optical output at a source-specific wavelength.

  2. 2. Power driver

    The source driver controls optical pulse timing and commanded power; a separate monitor checks delivered output.

  3. 3. Optical delivery

    Mirrors, lenses or a fiber guide and shape the beam. Spot area changes power density at the target.

  4. 4. Power sensor

    Power sensor connects to the acquisition or control chain. Optical energy is delivered in controlled pulses or continuous output. Source control, cooling, power sensing and interlocks supervise optical delivery.

  5. 5. Cooling system

    Temperature feedback and cooling remove heat from the source. Loss of cooling inhibits optical output.

  6. 6. Interlocks

    Door, cooling and output-readiness checks must be satisfied before a virtual optical event is enabled.

  7. 7. Controller

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  8. 8. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

Supervision: Cooling fault invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
