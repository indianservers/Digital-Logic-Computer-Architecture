Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Cycle a sample through controlled temperatures and monitor amplification through fluorescence. The PCR Machine signal path begins with thermal program and leads to amplification curve. Between these endpoints, temperature sensing, feedback controller, heater / cooler, fluorescence detector connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

Repeated denaturation, annealing and extension amplify a target DNA sequence. A temperature-feedback loop controls a Peltier thermal block; qPCR adds fluorescence monitoring.

  5. 5. Driver

    A driver converts a controller command into motion. Encoder, force or current feedback closes the loop and reveals stalled or overloaded operation.

  6. 6. Cooling fan

    Feedback-controlled heat transfer moves the reaction block toward its set point. Cooling and thermal contact affect the cycle response.

  7. 7. MCU

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  8. 8. Optical module (qPCR)

    An excitation source and fluorescence detector measure a reporter signal during qPCR cycles.

Supervision: Thermal sensor fault invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
