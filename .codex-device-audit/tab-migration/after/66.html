Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Thermocouples, pressure transducers, magnetic speed pickups and vibration sensors feed protected acquisition circuits. A monitoring processor filters and trends engine parameters, compares them with limits and sends indications, alerts and maintenance records. Example thresholds are instructional, not aircraft operating limits.

Convert engine measurements into trends, status indications and recorded alerts. The Aircraft Engine Monitoring System signal path begins with engine sensors and leads to engine indications. Between these endpoints, data acquisition, validation / calibration, trend / limit processing, aircraft data bus connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈How It Works — 6 Steps

  1. Measure temperature, pressure, vibration, fuel flow and speed.

  2. Amplify, filter and protect sensor signals.

  3. Convert analog inputs into sampled digital data.

  4. Compare values and trends with programmed limits.

  5. Show parameters and identify exceedances.

  6. Log time-aligned data for maintenance and recording.
