Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Convert incident X-rays into electronic pixel measurements for digital radiography. The Digital Radiography Detector signal path begins with x-ray photons and leads to digital projection. Between these endpoints, scintillator / conversion layer, pixel array, readout electronics, adc / correction connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

A scintillator and photodiode array, or a direct-conversion detector, convert X-ray interactions into pixel charge. Readout electronics, digitization and pixel corrections form the digital image.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Scintillator

    Incident radiation deposits energy in a scintillator, which emits optical photons for subsequent detection.

  2. 2. Photodiode array

    Incoming light produces an electrical response. Optical conversion or gain occurs before the analog signal is conditioned and sampled.

  3. 3. Thin-film transistors

    Thin-film transistors connects to the acquisition or control chain. A scintillator and photodiode array, or a direct-conversion detector, convert X-ray interactions into pixel charge. Readout electronics, digitization and pixel corrections form the digital image.

  4. 4. Readout electronics

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  5. 5. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  6. 6. Correction processor

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  7. 7. Battery

    The power source feeds regulated rails. Low supply voltage can reduce measurement reliability and must be detected by supervision.

    Wireless link connects to the acquisition or control chain. A scintillator and photodiode array, or a direct-conversion detector, convert X-ray interactions into pixel charge. Readout electronics, digitization and pixel corrections form the digital image.

Supervision: Dead detector pixels invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
