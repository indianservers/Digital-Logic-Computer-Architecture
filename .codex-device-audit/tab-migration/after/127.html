Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Harvest reader-field energy and exchange an identifier with an access-control reader. The RFID Access Card signal path begins with reader rf field and leads to reader validation. Between these endpoints, card antenna, rectifier / chip, rfid protocol, credential identifier connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

Detailed Operating Principle

Device overview

A passive LF proximity teaching card harvests energy from a 125 kHz reader field. Its chip sends an identifier through load modulation; the reader decodes it and an access controller checks local authorization before enabling a door relay. RFID covers several frequencies: this page models an LF card, not every credential technology pictured.

◈ Step-by-Step Working Principle

  1. The reader generates an alternating magnetic field.

  2. Mutual coupling induces card supply voltage.

  3. The chip operates only above its energy threshold.

  4. Changing the antenna load encodes the card identifier.

  5. The reader demodulates the response.

  6. Compare the recovered ID with local authorization.

  7. Enable the relay only after an authorized tap.
