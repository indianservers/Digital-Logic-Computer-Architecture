Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Coordinate gas delivery and ventilation while monitoring pressure, flow and gas concentrations. The Anesthesia Machine signal path begins with gas supply / settings and leads to monitoring display. Between these endpoints, flow / concentration sensing, acquisition, delivery supervision, gas / ventilation control connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

Gas-flow control, a vaporizer and a breathing circuit coordinate delivery. Oxygen sensing, pressure monitoring and capnography provide independent supervisory information.
