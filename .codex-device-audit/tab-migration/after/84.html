Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Near-infrared LEDs illuminate a scene. A day/night camera removes its IR-cut filter at night so a silicon image sensor can detect reflected near-IR light. Exposure, gain and noise reduction affect the output; this system is not a thermal imager.

Capture infrared scene information and convert it into a digital surveillance image. The Infrared Surveillance Camera signal path begins with infrared scene and leads to surveillance video. Between these endpoints, ir optics, sensitive image sensor, pixel acquisition, image correction connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈ Working Principle — Step by Step

  1. Switch the IR-cut filter and illumination mode.

  2. LEDs emit near-infrared light in night mode.

  3. The lens focuses reflected light on the image sensor.

  4. Pixel charge becomes digital image samples.

  5. Exposure, gain and denoising shape the display.

  6. Provide the processed video through the network interface.

  7. Display the live video or retain encoded frames on storage.

◈ Advantages, Limitations & Safety

• Near-IR illumination depends on reflectivity and distance.

• Gain amplifies noise as well as signal.

• Synthetic images are for learning, not surveillance evidence.
