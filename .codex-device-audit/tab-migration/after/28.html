Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Measure the maximum airflow reached during an exhalation and display the peak reading. The Peak Flow Meter signal path begins with exhaled airflow and leads to flow reading. Between these endpoints, flow sensor, signal conditioning, sampling, peak detection connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Mouthpiece

    Mouthpiece connects to the acquisition or control chain. A forceful expiration moves a mechanical indicator or generates an electronic airflow signal. Peak detection retains the maximum measured flow rather than total volume.

  2. 2. Airflow channel

    A pressure difference, turbine motion or thermal measurement estimates flow. Integrating flow over time gives volume.

  3. 3. Flow indicator

    Flow indicator connects to the acquisition or control chain. A forceful expiration moves a mechanical indicator or generates an electronic airflow signal. Peak detection retains the maximum measured flow rather than total volume.

  4. 4. Electronic sensor

    Electronic sensor connects to the acquisition or control chain. A forceful expiration moves a mechanical indicator or generates an electronic airflow signal. Peak detection retains the maximum measured flow rather than total volume.

  5. 5. Signal conditioning

    Bandwidth limiting removes unwanted components before sampling. An anti-alias filter suppresses frequencies above the acquisition bandwidth.

  6. 6. MCU

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  7. 7. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

Supervision: Flow channel blocked invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
