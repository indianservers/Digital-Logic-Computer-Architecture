Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Sequence cooking time and supervise door-state checks and power-control outputs. The Microwave Oven Controller signal path begins with keypad / time setting and leads to display / cycle feedback. Between these endpoints, door / temperature inputs, control mcu, timing / interlock logic, power control outputs connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

Detailed Operating Principle

Device overview

The keypad selects cooking time and power. A microcontroller checks door interlocks, then sequences magnetron power, turntable, fan and display. A conventional oven varies average heating with on/off duty cycles. This compressed-time model integrates absorbed power into a food thermal mass and inhibits heating when the door is open or the load is absent.

◈ How It Works — Step by Step

  1. Select time, average power and the teaching food mass.

  2. An open door or absent load inhibits the virtual magnetron.

  3. The MCU advances a compressed cooking timer.

  4. Duty cycling switches heating while the fan and turntable run.

  5. The thermal model integrates absorbed power and cooling loss.

  6. Heating stops at the selected duration and completion is indicated.
