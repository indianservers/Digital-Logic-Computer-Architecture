Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Fuse body motion and contact information to coordinate the movement of four articulated legs. The Quadruped Robot signal path begins with imu / joint / contact sensors and leads to balance feedback. Between these endpoints, body-state estimate, gait planner, joint control, leg actuators connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

Detailed Operating Principle

IMU, joint encoders, foot contacts and perception feed body-state estimation. A gait planner supplies foot trajectories; inverse kinematics converts them into joint targets and motor feedback tracks the motion. This reduced gait model computes two-link leg angles and alternating contact phases; body pitch and roll shift foot-height targets.

◈ Working Principle (Step by Step)

  1. Read orientation, joint and foot-contact measurements.

  2. Update the reduced body pose.

  3. Choose phase offsets and foot trajectories.

  4. Use inverse kinematics for the two-link legs.

  5. Send angles to virtual servo channels.

  6. Shift foot targets for body pitch and roll.

  7. Inspect contact sequence and reach limits.

◈ Key Takeaways

✓ Perception estimates body state and terrain

✓ Gait planning coordinates stance and swing phases

✓ Inverse kinematics converts foot targets to joint angles

✓ Joint feedback controls leg torque and position

✓ IMU and foot sensing support body balance

✓ Two-link kinematics omit full-body dynamics.

✓ Unreachable feet are flagged rather than fabricated.

✓ Virtual contact phase is not a physical stability guarantee.
