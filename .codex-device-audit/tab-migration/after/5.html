Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Use timed acoustic pulses and returning echoes to reconstruct tissue structure or motion. The Ultrasound Scanner signal path begins with pulse generator and leads to ultrasound image. Between these endpoints, piezoelectric array, returning echoes, receive beamformer, signal processing connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

A piezoelectric transducer emits sound and receives echoes. Echo delay estimates depth using d = ct/2; beamforming and amplitude processing form an image.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Transducer array

    A transducer converts electrical drive into sound and returning sound into voltage. Round-trip acoustic delay carries range information.

  2. 2. Pulse generator

    A timing state machine schedules pulses or watches for a sensed event. Period, width and event ordering define the output sequence.

  3. 3. Transmit / receive switch

    Transmit / receive switch connects to the acquisition or control chain. A piezoelectric transducer emits sound and receives echoes. Echo delay estimates depth using d = ct/2; beamforming and amplitude processing form an image.

  4. 4. Low-noise amplifier

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  5. 5. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  6. 6. Receive beamformer

    Timed delays align signals from multiple acoustic channels. Their sum emphasizes echoes arriving from a selected direction and depth.

  7. 7. DSP

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  8. 8. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

Supervision: Poor acoustic coupling invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
