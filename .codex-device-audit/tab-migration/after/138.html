Operating Principle & Engineering Context

A current transformer or shunt and a voltage-sensing network feed isolated conditioning and anti-alias filters. A metering ADC samples voltage and current; instantaneous products are averaged to obtain real power and integrated to obtain energy. The MCU stores readings and manages display, communication, tamper status and a disconnect relay. This lab computes actual sampled sinusoidal voltage and current.

Sample voltage and current to calculate power and accumulate energy use. The Smart Energy Meter signal path begins with voltage / current sensors and leads to display / communication. Between these endpoints, analog front end, adc, power calculation, energy accumulator connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈ How It Works — Step by Step

  1. Acquire synthetic voltage and current waveforms.

  2. Sample both channels at a common clock.

  3. Average sample products for real power and derive RMS values.

  4. Integrate real power over the selected interval.

  5. Publish a local meter record to the communication display.

  6. Report tamper events and honor relay disconnection.
