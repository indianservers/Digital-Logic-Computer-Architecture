Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Transform a desired pose into coordinated joint motion using motor feedback. The Robotic Arm signal path begins with target pose and leads to position feedback. Between these endpoints, inverse kinematics, trajectory planner, servo drives, joint motors / encoders connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

Inverse kinematics maps a desired tool pose to joints. Servo loops compare commanded and measured joint motion to adjust motor drive currents.
