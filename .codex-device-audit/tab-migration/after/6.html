Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Between these endpoints, rf receive coil, signal acquisition, adc, reconstruction computer connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

A main magnetic field aligns nuclear spins. RF excitation, gradient encoding and receive-coil signals provide data for spatial image reconstruction.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Main magnet

    The static magnetic field establishes the equilibrium spin alignment and sets the proton Larmor frequency.

  2. 2. Gradient coils

    Controlled field gradients make resonance frequency and phase depend on spatial position, enabling slice selection and image encoding.

  3. 3. RF transmitter

    A timed radio-frequency pulse tips magnetization. Its frequency and duration cooperate with gradient fields to select the excited region.

  4. 4. RF receive coil

    The coil receives weak radio-frequency signals from precessing magnetization. The receive chain amplifies and digitizes them for reconstruction.

  5. 5. Gradient amplifiers

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  6. 6. Receive chain

    Receive chain connects to the acquisition or control chain. A main magnetic field aligns nuclear spins. RF excitation, gradient encoding and receive-coil signals provide data for spatial image reconstruction.

  7. 7. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  8. 8. Reconstruction computer

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

Supervision: Receive-coil fault invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
