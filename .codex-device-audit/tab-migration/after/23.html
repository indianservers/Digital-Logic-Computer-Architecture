Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Acquire small electrical potentials from scalp electrodes and present synchronized brain-activity traces. The EEG Machine signal path begins with scalp electrodes and leads to eeg traces. Between these endpoints, differential amplifier, filtering, adc, multichannel analysis connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

Scalp electrodes measure small voltage differences associated with populations of neurons. Band-limited amplification and sampling reveal delta, theta, alpha and beta activity.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Electrode cap

    Conductive contacts acquire a differential biopotential or provide the supervised output interface. Contact impedance affects signal quality.

  2. 2. Input protection

    Input protection and interlocks supervise the interface and prevent invalid states from propagating to the controlled output.

  3. 3. Differential amplifier

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  4. 4. Band-pass filter

    Bandwidth limiting removes unwanted components before sampling. An anti-alias filter suppresses frequencies above the acquisition bandwidth.

  5. 5. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  6. 6. DSP

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  7. 7. Memory

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

  8. 8. Display

    The interface presents the processed quantity or stores acquired data with its timing and validity state.

Supervision: High electrode impedance invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.

◈Circuit View

The sensor interface conditions the analog signal. Sampling and digital processing extract the device’s useful output.
