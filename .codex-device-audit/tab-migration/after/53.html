Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Turn sound into digital samples for filtering, analysis or controlled amplification. The Hearing Aid signal path begins with sound and leads to speaker / recording. Between these endpoints, microphone, preamplifier, adc, audio dsp connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

A microphone captures sound for digitization. Frequency-dependent DSP gain and noise reduction adapt the signal before a miniature receiver reproduces audio.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Microphone

    A miniature acoustic transducer converts pressure fluctuations into an electrical signal for amplification and sampling.

  2. 2. Preamp

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  3. 3. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  4. 4. DSP

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  5. 5. Frequency-dependent gain

    Frequency-dependent gain amplifies selected spectral bands. Gain in dB describes a logarithmic amplitude relationship.

  6. 6. DAC

    The DAC and miniature acoustic receiver turn processed samples into sound at the ear.

  7. 7. Receiver

    The DAC and miniature acoustic receiver turn processed samples into sound at the ear.

  8. 8. Battery

    The power source feeds regulated rails. Low supply voltage can reduce measurement reliability and must be detected by supervision.

Supervision: Microphone fault invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
