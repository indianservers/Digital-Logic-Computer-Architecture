Between these endpoints, chest-piece microphone, low-noise amplifier, adc, audio filtering connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

The chestpiece couples body sounds into a microphone or piezoelectric sensor. Low-noise amplification, filtering and digitization prepare a phonocardiogram or audio signal for listening and recording.

Build This Device — System Architecture
  1. 1. Chestpiece diaphragm

    Chestpiece diaphragm connects to the acquisition or control chain. The chestpiece couples body sounds into a microphone or piezoelectric sensor. Low-noise amplification, filtering and digitization prepare a phonocardiogram or audio signal for listening and recording.

  2. 2. Acoustic sensor

    Acoustic sensor connects to the acquisition or control chain. The chestpiece couples body sounds into a microphone or piezoelectric sensor. Low-noise amplification, filtering and digitization prepare a phonocardiogram or audio signal for listening and recording.

  3. 3. Preamplifier

    A high-input-impedance front end amplifies the useful signal while reducing common-mode interference. Gain and headroom determine the sampling range.

  4. 4. Analog filter

    Bandwidth limiting removes unwanted components before sampling. An anti-alias filter suppresses frequencies above the acquisition bandwidth.

  5. 5. ADC

    An analog-to-digital converter quantizes sampled voltage into binary codes. Reference voltage, resolution and sample timing affect the result.

  6. 6. DSP

    Firmware or a digital processor applies calibration, validates signal quality, computes the useful quantity and supervises the system state.

  7. 7. Bluetooth

    Bluetooth connects to the acquisition or control chain. The chestpiece couples body sounds into a microphone or piezoelectric sensor. Low-noise amplification, filtering and digitization prepare a phonocardiogram or audio signal for listening and recording.

  8. 8. Battery

    The power source feeds regulated rails. Low supply voltage can reduce measurement reliability and must be detected by supervision.

  9. 9. Audio output

    A digital-to-analog interface and transducer reproduce the processed audio waveform. Output limits and gain shape the audible signal.

Supervision: Chestpiece disconnected invalidates the affected signal chain. The processor must check signal quality before presenting a result or enabling an output.
