Operating Principle & Engineering Context

Magnify a specimen optically and digitize its image for display and measurement. The Digital Microscope signal path begins with illumination and leads to magnified display. Between these endpoints, specimen / objective, image sensor, pixel readout, image processing connect the physical system to its digital processing and output.

◈Detailed Operating Principle

Key principle

An objective and tube lens magnify an illuminated specimen onto an image sensor. Focus, aperture, sampling and contrast processing govern the detail visible on the display.
